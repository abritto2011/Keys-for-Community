About Keys for Community

Our Story & Mission

Mission Statement

Our mission is to bring joy, connection, and emotional warmth to senior living communities through live piano performances by children of all ages and abilities. We create a supportive space where young musicians can grow in confidence, develop empathy, and share their talents with others. By bridging generations through music, we nurture meaningful relationships and inspire a culture of kindness and service.

Vision Statement

We envision a world where music serves as a bridge between generations, fostering understanding, compassion, and shared joy. Our long-term vision is to build a vibrant network of young performers who regularly bring uplifting musical experiences to senior communities across the region.

How It Works

Three Simple Steps

1

Students Sign Up

Young pianists register online, share their experience level, and note their availability.

2

We Match & Prepare

Our founders pair students with nearby senior living facilities and help them prepare a program.

3

Music Happens

Students perform live for seniors, earn community service hours, and build cross-generational friendships.
